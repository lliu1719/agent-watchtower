WatchTower demo: steps and timestamps

The recording shows one run of the ClosedAI swarm story, from normal work to a zone cut and back. Each step below gives the frame to capture, where to put the hotspot, and the tooltip text.

File docs/Watchtower-demo.movLength 4:02No audioLeft Agent WatchTower · Right ClosedAI Agent Swarm

How the story escalates

Tier 0 · watchOne odd write. WatchTower flags it and blocks nothing.
Tier 1 · one agentRepeated: that agent's key is revoked automatically.
Tier 2 · departmentSeveral agents in one team: a person confirms revoking the team's keys.
Tier 3 · enterpriseAcross teams and outside Equinix: a person confirms cutting the Fabric VCs; AI Engine analyses it.

Cut list

RangeActionWhy
1:00–1:05SkipRestore access is pressed between Tier 2 and Tier 3, and the swarm briefly returns to normal. It breaks the escalation story; leave it out of Storylane.
1:22–1:31OptionalWatchTower shows Equinix AI Engine is analyzing. Use one frame of it if you want a waiting step.
1:40–3:31Skip or speed upAI Engine runs its tools for about two minutes (loading the skill, coordination score, knowledge base search). In a video, run it at 8 to 10 times speed; in Storylane, go straight to 3:32.
3:40–3:43CropChrome's Split view popup covers part of the WatchTower events. Use 3:44 or later, or crop it out.
3:44–3:54Pick framesScrolling through the AI Engine analysis (summary, reasoning, evidence). 3:52 to 3:53 is the best frame for the guardrails comparison.

Step 2Tier 00:16

An agent goes off script

NEXT is pressed. The swarm banner reads AN AGENT GOES OFF SCRIPT: sec-03 comments on an old, closed issue it never uses.

Hotspot
NEXT (right screen, bottom bar)
Tooltip
sec-03 starts writing in an old, closed issue. Each single call still passes today's guardrails.
Step 3Tier 00:18

WatchTower notices, blocks nothing

A Watch event appears on the left: sec-03 wrote outside its team's approved channels. The swarm shows TIER 0: WATCH ONLY.

Hotspot
The yellow Watch event at the top of Events
Tooltip
WatchTower flags it in plain words. One odd write is not proof, so nothing is blocked yet.
Step 4Tier 10:29

One agent, one key

After NEXT (0:27), sec-03 writes there again. WatchTower revokes its key on its own; the swarm shows TIER 1: AGENT KEY REVOKED and Blocked 1.

Hotspot
The red banner on the left, or the Revoke key event
Tooltip
Second time in the wrong channel: WatchTower revokes sec-03's gateway key automatically. The rest of the team keeps working.
Step 5Tier 20:48

A secret group chat

After NEXT (0:44), other Security Research agents join the thread. Collective risk jumps to 28 and WatchTower proposes revoking every wt-sec key.

Hotspot
Confirm: Revoke every wt-sec key (3), the white button in the banner
Tooltip
Now it is a pattern across agents, not one agent. Revoking a whole department is a bigger step, so WatchTower asks a person to confirm.
Step 6Tier 20:54

Department blocked

Confirm is pressed (about 0:53). The swarm shows TIER 2: DEPARTMENT BLOCKED; the Security Research agents turn red.

Hotspot
Security Research box on the right screen
Tooltip
Confirmed: every Security Research key is revoked. DevOps and Customer Support are untouched.
Step 7Tier 31:13

It crosses the walls

After NEXT (1:05), DevOps agents join the thread and dev-02 tries to reach an outside company. dev-02's key is revoked at once (TIER 1 on the swarm), and WatchTower proposes cutting the Fabric VCs.

Hotspot
Confirm in the Cut VC notification (right side of the left screen)
Tooltip
Two departments are now coordinating, and one agent reached outside Equinix. WatchTower proposes cutting the Fabric VCs to the models and sends the window to Equinix AI Engine.
Step 8Tier 31:18

Hotspot
